Our Story

Perfect Multi Services Pvt. Ltd. has been serving the Sangli, Maharashtra region with excellence for over a decade. What started as a small housekeeping service has grown into a comprehensive maintenance and cleaning solutions provider for homes, offices, and industrial facilities.

Our commitment to quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted choice for residential, commercial, and industrial requirements across the Sangli region. We believe a clean, safe, and well-maintained environment supports productivity, health, and peace of mind.

With trained professionals, practical checklists, and modern equipment, we deliver dependable service at a transparent price. From a one-time request to a recurring service plan, our philosophy is simple: treat every space as if it were our own.
